# How to use Butsó

Butsó is free to join. These guides cover the main things you can do. The same guides, as step-by-step walkthroughs, are in the app under **Help & Support**.

> This file is generated from `lib/guideContent.ts`. Edit that file, then run `npx tsx scripts/make-guide-md.ts`.

## Join and set up your profile
*For: Everyone.* Free to join, for people 18 and over. Takes about three minutes.

1. Open Butsó and tap Join. Sign up with your email, or with Google.
2. Confirm your date of birth and that you are 18 or over, and agree to the Terms and Privacy Policy. Your date of birth stays private.
3. Pick what you want to do first: find work or hire people. You can always do both later.
4. Pick a username. People see @username, not your real name, until you share your contact details. A phone number is optional, and we do not check it yet.
5. Add your area, your skills and a short bio. Workers with a complete profile who start applying are shown to employers first for 30 days.

## Find work and apply
*For: Workers.* Apply early. Each job takes at most 30 applicants.

1. Tap Jobs. Use the search box to look for a skill or a place, or scroll the list.
2. Tap a job to read the details: what is needed, the budget, and where.
3. Tap Apply. Add a short note, and the price you expect if you want to.
4. The employer is notified and picks from the people who applied. You will get a notification.
5. When you are chosen, you and the employer can see each other’s contact details.

## Post a job and hire someone
*For: Employers.* Free to post. You choose who you hire.

1. From your dashboard, tap Post Job.
2. Write a clear title and description. Choose a category, the budget and the location.
3. Workers apply, and you get a notification each time. Open your job to see everyone who applied.
4. Check each applicant’s skills, rating and reviews. Tap Hire on the one you choose.
5. You can also hire someone directly: open their profile, tap Hire this person, and pick one of your open jobs. They accept or decline.
6. Need to change something? Edit, close or reopen your job any time. A job with a hired worker cannot be deleted.

## Set up your shop
*For: Shop owners.* Let customers find you, see your work and book you.

1. Open My Shop and start setting up. Add your shop name and pick a category.
2. Add a shop photo, your area, and a short description of what you do.
3. Your phone number is hidden by default. You can choose to show it, or stay private.
4. Add your services with prices, and your opening hours. Then save your shop.
5. Open your shop page and tap the Work tab. Add up to 12 photos of your work, with a short caption.
6. Tap Share to send your shop link on WhatsApp.

## Book a service
*For: Customers.* Pick a shop, pick a time, and agree the price directly.

1. Tap Shops and find a barber, salon, tailor or repair shop near you.
2. Look at their work photos and reviews, then choose a service.
3. Choose a date and time, then send your booking.
4. The shop accepts or declines. You both see the update in Bookings.
5. After the job is marked complete, you can leave a review. You pay the shop directly for now.

## Messages, privacy and staying safe
*For: Everyone.* Your real name and phone number stay hidden until you decide.

1. To contact someone new, send a message request of up to 200 characters. Say who you are and what you need.
2. The other person sees it in their Requests tab. When they accept, a chat opens.
3. Inside a chat, you can ask to see their contact details. They choose whether to share.
4. Hiring someone, or accepting a booking, shares contact details both ways automatically.
5. Someone bothering you? Open the chat menu and tap Block or Report. They cannot contact you again.

## Fees

Butsó is free to join, free to post and free to apply while we grow. When secure in-app payments launch, we may take a small cut from the person who earns, never from the person hiring, and only on payments made through the app. We will give you at least 30 days’ notice before any fee starts. For now, agree the price directly and pay each other directly. Only pay when you are happy with the work.
